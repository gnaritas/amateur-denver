Privacy

What this site knows about you

Very little, on purpose.

The contact form

When you send the contact form, Amateur receives your name, message, and any phone number or email you add — used only to answer you. Messages are delivered securely and kept for a reasonable period, then discarded.

No trackers, no ads

This site loads no third-party scripts, no advertising pixels and no analytics cookies. Nothing follows you from this page to anywhere else. The only cookies on this site are set by the map preview embed on the contact page, governed by the mapped provider’s own privacy policy.

Your choices

Reply to any message from Amateur with “unsubscribe” to stop mailings, or ask via the contact form to see or delete anything the site holds about you.

Questions about this page? The contact form works for those too.
