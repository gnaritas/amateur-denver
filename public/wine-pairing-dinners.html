The kitchen

David cooks

David Arnold focuses on whole-ingredient cooking built on local farms — the produce-first, waste-conscious hand behind Freehand Provisions’ farm dinners, after years as executive sous chef at Uchi and at the chef counter inside Odell’s Bagels.

Expect plates that use an ingredient end to end, in season, from farms he buys from by name.

The glass

Hannah pours

Hannah Cunningham built Bon Vin near Sloan’s Lake on independent winemakers using sustainable practices, and made the neighborhood’s wine calendar with tastings, classes and food pop-ups outside the shop. Every flight beside these five courses comes from that list.

Pairing, here, means wine that makes the food taste like itself.

How the nights run

“Amateur is not a prix-fixe concept; however, as a two-night pop-up within another establishment, a fixed menu allows us to present Amateur’s cuisine as we intend it to be experienced.”

One sitting menu, five passes, both nights — hosted at Odell’s Bagels while the permanent West Highlands space comes together.

Before you book

When and where are the dinners?

Monday, December 7th and Tuesday, December 8th, 2026, hosted at Odell’s Bagels in Denver.

What does dinner cost?

The Tuesday, December 8 dinner is $95 per guest with an optional wine pairing for an additional $45. Tickets for both nights sell on the official ticket page.

Is Amateur open as a restaurant yet?

Not yet — the founders are finalizing a West Highlands space with a Summer 2027 opening targeted. These dinners are the public preview.

How do tickets work?

Both preview nights sell through Amateur’s official event page — the “Get tickets” links on this site go straight there.
