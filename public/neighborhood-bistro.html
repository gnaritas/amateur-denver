Targeting Summer 2027 · West Highlands

A neighborhood bistro with a serious wine list

Chef-driven plates built around produce, poured beside independent wine — in the part of Denver that keeps asking for exactly this.

Follow the openingOr taste it first at dinner

The gap in the map

“There’s a serious gap in the market in the West Highland, Sloans Lake area… Once a week since we opened, I’ve had customers say how much the neighborhood needs a good chef-driven restaurant.”

— Hannah Cunningham, in Westword

What the room will be

Small and walkable

No bigger than 50 seats, in a relaxed neighborhood a short walk or ride from Bon Vin — a room you can tell your neighbors about.

Plates from the farm in

Seasonal, chef-driven cooking designed around produce, with whole-ingredient use and local farms at the center — David’s discipline from Freehand Provisions.

Wine from people, not brands

A list of independent winemakers using sustainable practices, priced to drink, guided by Hannah’s pairing work and Bon Vin’s shelves.

Reasonably priced, seriously good

The founders’ own words: “really great food and reasonably priced, but amazing, wine” — a bistro, not an event.

The room, being drawn

Design & architecture by Regular Architecture

The space is being finalized now; the design and architecture for it are in the hands of Regular Architecture — announced on Amateur’s own project pages.

Beyond service

A wine calendar, too

The bar side carries Hannah’s track record — tastings, pairing classes and food pop-ups that have already anchored Sloan’s Lake’s calendar will continue under Amateur’s roof.

West Highlands sits between Sloan’s Lake and the Highlands — the neighborhood Amateur is targeting for its Summer 2027 opening. A street address will be announced when the space is signed.
