Why the name

Amateur, from amare, “to love”

“The literal Latin translation of amateur is someone who does something for the love or passion of it. You don’t really get into food because you plan to make a lot of money. You do it because you love the thing.” — chef David Arnold, as told to Westword

Both partners left careers in software — Hannah as a software designer, David as a software engineer — to chase hospitality full-time.
